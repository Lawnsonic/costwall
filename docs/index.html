How it works

The break-even move

Crossing the spread to get in, crossing it again to get out, the taker fee both ways, any builder fee both ways, and funding over the hold when it runs against you. Entry crossing uses the current book at your size. APPROVED means the cost policy passed, not that profit is expected. Exit crossing assumes similar liquidity later, and funding projects the current rate over the hold.

The gateway

An agent connected to Costwall never holds a trading key. It asks the gateway to trade; the gateway prices the trade, and only an approval signed by the oracle and still unexpired becomes an order, sent as a limit at the approved price. Reduce-only closes bypass the cost policy, but still depend on liquidity and exchange acceptance.

Stock markets after hours

When the underlying exchange is closed, trade.xyz prices its stock perps from its own order book inside discovery bounds. What a fill is worth when the real market reopens cannot be measured, so Costwall refuses unless you say otherwise.

This page runs a browser copy of the Python oracle in the repository, at Hyperliquid's public base fee tier. APPROVED means the cost policy passed, not that profit is expected. Exit crossing assumes the exit book looks like today's; favourable funding is shown but never counted in your favour. Nothing on this page can place an order.
