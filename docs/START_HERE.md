Use historical evidence with its date shown. No fresh trade is needed.
Call the builder-fee business model proposed, and hosted MCP pending.
Internal scanner rows are test observations, not customers or avoided losses.

## Vocabulary
- Basis point: 0.01%; ten basis points is 0.1%.
- Break-even estimate: movement needed to cover the model's costs.
- IOC: fill available size inside the limit immediately, cancel the rest.
- Reduce-only: reduce an existing position without opening the opposite one.
- Funding scenario: a projection from the current rate, not a guaranteed bill.
- Local gateway: the program holding the trading key on the operator's machine.
